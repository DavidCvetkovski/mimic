Across your devices

The browser and Mac app share the same voice library when they use the same Mimic engine. iPhone runs its own engine and keeps its library on the phone.

Use Export in Voices to transfer a .mimicvoice file through the encrypted cloud library. Pair the iPhone and Mac apps in Settings for automatic voice sync. All three offer the same starter passages. The optional “Write me one” assistant is currently available on iPhone.
